Analysis of the 60 daily networks

How did the network change?

Overall connectivity. The mean number of active links rose from 5.50 per day in days 1–20 to 7.25 in days 41–60. Days 45, 51, and 57 each had ten links involving all twelve companies. Greater activity did not produce a fully connected network on those days.

Company activity. Fusion Electronics was most active in days 21–40, with 53 incident daily links and $1.05 million in associated transaction value. In days 41–60, Ion Systems led with 46 incident links and $1.10 million, followed by Cedar Retail with 43 links and $0.90 million.

Separate clusters. On day 1, the network consisted of three disconnected pairs and six companies with no transaction. On day 42, all twelve companies were active, but the largest connected component contained eight companies. The network therefore remained partitioned even during a relatively active period.

Changing relationships. BlueRiver Logistics and Delta Components transacted on ten even-numbered days through day 20, then did not appear together again. Fusion Electronics and Juniper Stores first appeared together on day 22. The mean value of Fusion Electronics–Granite Wholesale transactions increased from about $8,202 in days 1–20 to $25,003 in days 21–40.

Cross-region exchange. Cross-region links accounted for 70 of 110 observed links (63.6%) in days 1–20 and 105 of 145 (72.4%) in days 41–60. The later period was more cross-regional by this measure. Region is shown by company color, so these connections can also be inspected day by day.
