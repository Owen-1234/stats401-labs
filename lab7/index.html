Five temporal-network questions

What the daily networks show

Connectivity increased. Daily active links averaged 5.50 in days 1–20 and 7.25 in days 41–60. Days 45, 51, and 57 each had ten links involving all twelve companies, although none formed a single connected component.

Commercial activity shifted. Fusion Electronics was incident to 53 daily links worth $1.05 million in days 21–40. In days 41–60, Ion Systems led with 46 daily links worth $1.10 million, followed by Cedar Retail with 43 links worth $0.90 million.

Separate clusters persisted. Day 1 had three disconnected pairs and six inactive companies. On day 42, all twelve companies were active, but the connected components still had eight, two, and two companies.

Relationships changed in presence and strength. BlueRiver Logistics and Delta Components transacted on ten even-numbered days through day 20 and did not appear together later. Fusion Electronics and Juniper Stores first appeared together on day 22. Fusion Electronics–Granite Wholesale transactions averaged $8,202 in days 1–20 and $25,003 in days 21–40.

Cross-region exchange became more common. Cross-region links represented 70 of 110 daily links (63.6%) in days 1–20 and 105 of 145 (72.4%) in days 41–60. Company color shows region, so individual cross-region links can be inspected in the network.
